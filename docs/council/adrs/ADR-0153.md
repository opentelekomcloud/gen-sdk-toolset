The reason for a document exclusion is not specified and not stored, because a document exclusion can be cancelled under ADR-0151, and an excluded document stays visible in the list with the "Excluded" mark under ADR-0152. So exclusion remains a quick action without an extra step, and we accept that other users will not know why the document was removed from the scan.
