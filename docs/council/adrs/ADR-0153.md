The reason for a document exclusion is neither given nor stored, because under ADR-0151 a document exclusion can be undone, and under ADR-0152 an excluded document is visible in the list marked "Excluded", so a mistaken exclusion is easy to notice and fix without an explanation. Without a reason, excluding has no extra step, and the mark on the snapshot document from ADR-0155 needs no reason field.
