Excluded documents stay in the common document list on the service page in Scan, marked "Excluded", and can be selected with a separate filter, because the user sees an excluded document in its usual place among the service documents, and the filter collects all exclusions of the service in one list. Under ADR-0154 and ADR-0155 an excluded document stays in the active snapshot with an exclusion mark, so showing exclusions needs no separate data source.
