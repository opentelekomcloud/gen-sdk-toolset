Excluded documents stay in the general list of documents on the service page in Scan with the "Excluded" mark, and they can be selected with a separate filter, because the user sees an excluded document in its usual place among the documents of the service, and the filter selects all exclusions of this service at once. Under ADR-0154 the scanner still parses the document, so it stays among the documents of the active snapshot, and the mark can be shown on the same Scan page where the document is excluded under ADR-0147 and where it is convenient to cancel the exclusion under ADR-0151.
