Manual exclusion is not related to exclusion by configuration: the scanner still parses the document, and the panel hides it when reading the active snapshot, does not count it in the data of the active snapshot, and shows it in the Scan document list with the "Excluded" mark; the preliminary layout includes all endpoints of the non-excluded documents of the service from the active snapshot, regardless of their status, because manual exclusion works only in the panel. So it takes effect immediately, without a new scan, as ADR-0148 requires, and after cancellation under ADR-0151 the document takes part in the scan again: the scanner parses it anyway. The global excluded_segments setting of the scanner does not change, and no channel is needed to pass the settings of a single service to the scanner. There is no such channel now: scan_repository accepts only repo and branch.
