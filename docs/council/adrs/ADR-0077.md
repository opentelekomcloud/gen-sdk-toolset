A visible panel tab shows the new state no later than 15 seconds after the change is saved on the server; in the tab where a scan, a snapshot activation, a service exclusion or an exclusion undo was performed, the Generation list refreshes right after that action, because this deadline is met by periodically refetching the displayed data per ADR-0076, without long-lived connections. The summary and attention queries, each of which recomputes the state of all services, then run much less often than with a 2-second deadline, which makes it easier to stay within the load limit of ADR-0084. A single deadline for all change sources from ADR-0079 is easier to verify with tests than separate deadlines for panel actions and for discovery and the CLI.

Supersedes: ADR-0048.
