While resource generation is waiting to start or running on at least one target, the resource's field types cannot be fixed, its grouping cannot be edited, and the kind and SDK method name of its operations cannot be changed; after it finishes, successfully or not, these edits are allowed again, and the user edits the resource grouping directly in the editor, without the workaround through a new resource, because the kind and SDK method name are part of the operation model that is saved with the job at start under ADR-0114. So they are generation inputs just like field types and grouping, and one locking rule for all these edits keeps the specification on the page from diverging from the model of the running job.

Supersedes: ADR-0111.
