The backend computes the selection of services for the Generation list from stored Scan data, and the frontend shows the ready result, because the selection rule relies on `endpoints_total` of the active snapshot, and this field exists only on the server. This way the rule lives in one place, next to the other derived service states, and is not duplicated on the client.
