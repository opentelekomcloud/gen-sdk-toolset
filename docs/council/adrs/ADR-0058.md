The preliminary grouping includes all endpoints of the service from the active Scan snapshot, whatever their status, because the user sees in the editor everything the scanner found and assigns endpoints with errors themselves, and ADR-0067 does not allow confirming a resource until all its endpoints are in status OK. Endpoints without an API version and without a constant URI segment already have their own lists under ADR-0059 and ADR-0057, and after a rescan ADR-0071 also adds new endpoints without filtering them by status.
