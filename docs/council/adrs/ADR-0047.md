In this task, rows of the /generation list do not link to the service card or nested pages, and a direct URL of /generation/:name or of the nested pages for a service from the new list shows PageNotFound, because this cuts the scope of the current stage to showing the list from Scan. Generation pages have no real data for services from Scan, so they must not be shown with substituted mock data. The existing PageNotFound covers that without a new page state. The card and nested pages come back when real generation data is connected.
