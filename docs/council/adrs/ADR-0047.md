In this task, rows of the /generation list do not link to the service card or nested pages, and a direct URL of /generation/:name or of the nested pages for a service from the new list shows PageNotFound, because the task is limited to showing the list from Scan (F3). Generation pages have no real data for services from Scan, so they must not be shown with substituted mock data.
