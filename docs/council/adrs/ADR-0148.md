A document exclusion takes effect right after confirmation, without a new scan: the document is no longer counted in the active snapshot data; endpoints of non-excluded documents of the active snapshot go into the preliminary grouping regardless of their status, because the user sees the result of the exclusion at once, does not wait for a scan to finish, and each exclusion does not require a full scan of the service. This is consistent with ADR-0154 and ADR-0155: the mark is stored on the snapshot document, and the panel does not count a marked document when reading the active snapshot. What happens to the layout and the edits is defined by ADR-0158 and ADR-0159.

Supersedes: ADR-0058, ADR-0041.
