Document exclusion takes effect immediately after confirmation, without a new scan: the document is no longer counted in the data of the active snapshot; endpoints of non-excluded documents of the active snapshot still go into the preliminary grouping regardless of their status, because the user sees the result immediately and does not wait for a full scan of the service for each exclusion, and other tabs and users will see the change through the periodic re-read under ADR-0076. Under ADR-0162 the exclusion is stored outside snapshots, and under ADR-0154 the panel applies it when reading the active snapshot, so it needs no new snapshot and the snapshots themselves do not change.

Supersedes: ADR-0058, ADR-0041.
