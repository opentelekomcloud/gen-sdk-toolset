Each field whose nested structure comes from a table with a duplicate anchor is marked in the specification with the warning “first of several tables used”, with links to the documents of the chosen and the dropped tables. The field does not count as a problem field because of this, because the user sees a possible substitution of the nested structure right at the field they check before generation. Next to the link to the source table from ADR-0102, they can open and compare the documents of the chosen and the dropped tables. The field does not become a problem field under ADR-0100 and therefore does not block starting generation under ADR-0106.
