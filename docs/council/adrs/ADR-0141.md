While generation of at least one resource of the service is waiting to start or running, the service's layout cannot be replaced: the server responds with 409. After generation finishes, successfully or not, replacement is available again, because a repeated layout by ADR-0136 resets all resources of the service at once. So the ban in ADR-0132 on editing a resource's layout during its generation applies to the whole service, and the model saved with a started task does not diverge from the layout on the page. A generation that has already started does not lose its work, and the new layout then lays out all resources again, including the one that was being generated.
