A service gets into the Generation list only if its active snapshot has at least one endpoint from non-excluded documents, because the Generation list should keep only services that have something to generate, and under ADR-0156 endpoints of excluded documents do not get into the layout. Exclusion of a whole service under ADR-0043 works the same way: the user does not see in Generation what they removed from Scan.

Supersedes: ADR-0041.
