A service gets into the Generation list only if its active snapshot has at least one endpoint from non-excluded documents, because only services that have something to generate stay in the Generation list. Under ADR-0156 endpoints of excluded documents do not get into the layout, and under ADR-0148 an exclusion takes effect in the active snapshot data at once. As with a service exclusion under ADR-0043, the user does not see in Generation what they removed in Scan.

Supersedes: ADR-0041.
