In /generation/:name links a service is addressed by its full `repo` in owner/name format, the same identifier as in Scan, because the future service card will need it: `repo` is unique and already serves as the service identifier in Scan, so a link can be matched to Scan data without a mapping table and without changing the API. Until the card exists, these URLs show PageNotFound (ADR-0047).
