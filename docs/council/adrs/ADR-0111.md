While a resource's generation is waiting to start or running on at least one target, its field types cannot be fixed and its grouping cannot be edited; after it finishes, successfully or not, fixing is allowed again, and the user edits the resource's grouping directly in the editor, without a workaround through a new resource, because the selected types are the same generation input as the resource's grouping. So one clear locking rule applies to both edits, and while the job runs, the specification on the page does not diverge from the model that ADR-0114 saves at start.

Supersedes: ADR-0069.
