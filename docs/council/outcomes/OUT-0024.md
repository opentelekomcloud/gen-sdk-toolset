The service card and nested pages are not available for services from the new list

Rows of the /generation list do not link to the service card or to the nested pages. In the URL /generation/:name, a service is addressed by its full `repo` in the owner/name format, as in Scan. A direct URL to the card or to the nested pages layout, spec and result for a service from the new list shows PageNotFound. Mock data is not filled in for such a service.

Acceptance criteria:
- Clicking a service row in the /generation list opens neither the service card nor the nested pages.
- Going directly to the card URL /generation/:name, where a service from the list is given by its `repo` in the owner/name format, shows PageNotFound.
- Going directly to the URL of the layout, spec or result page for a service from the list, given by its `repo`, shows PageNotFound.
- None of these pages shows resources, specs or results from mock data for a service from the new list.
