Scan data defines which services are in the Generation list

The backend selects the Generation service list from the stored Scan data and returns it ready to use. The /generation page shows this result without doing its own selection. The list includes only services from the Scan set. Excluded services and services that are not in the Scan list do not get into it. A service is in the list if `endpoints_total` in its active snapshot is greater than zero. It is the active snapshot that counts, not the latest one. A service with no active snapshot or with `endpoints_total` equal to zero is not in the list. A running scan does not affect the list: during a scan, a service is selected by the same conditions as without a scan. A service that does not yet have a matching active snapshot appears in the list only after such a snapshot becomes active. After an exclusion is undone, the service is checked again against the common selection condition. The list does not hide services by generation state: all services that pass the selection are shown. Services that exist only in the Generation mock data and are missing from the Scan data do not get into the list.

Acceptance criteria:
- A service whose active snapshot has `endpoints_total` greater than zero is in the Generation list returned by the backend and on the /generation page.
- A service whose active snapshot has `endpoints_total` equal to zero is not in the list, including when its Scan status is scanned.
- A service with no active snapshot is not in the list.
- If a snapshot other than the latest one is pinned as active, the selection uses the active snapshot.
- A service with a matching active snapshot that is being scanned stays in the list for the whole scan.
- A service that is scanned for the first time and does not yet have a matching active snapshot is not in the list until such a snapshot becomes active.
- A scan that ended with an error does not change whether the service is in the list.
- A service excluded from Scan is not in the list, even if `endpoints_total` in its active snapshot is greater than zero.
- After an exclusion is undone, a service with a matching active snapshot is in the list again, and a service without such a snapshot is not.
- A service that is not in the Scan service list is not in the Generation list either.
- No service that passed the selection is hidden on the /generation page because of its generation state.
- The set of services on the /generation page matches the set returned by the backend.
- The list has no services that exist only in the Generation mock data and are missing from the Scan data.
