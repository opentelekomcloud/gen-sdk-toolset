The list updates after Scan data changes in the panel

After a scan, a snapshot activation, a service exclusion or an exclusion undo, the /generation list shows the new state of the Scan data right away. No page reload is needed for this.

Acceptance criteria:
- If a scan changed whether a service matches the selection condition, after the scan the service appears in or disappears from the /generation list without a page reload.
- If a snapshot activation changed whether a service matches the selection condition, the service appears in or disappears from the list without a page reload.
- After a service is excluded from Scan, it disappears from the /generation list without a page reload.
- After an exclusion is undone, a service with a matching active snapshot appears in the /generation list without a page reload.
