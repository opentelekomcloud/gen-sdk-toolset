Endpoints of one version are first grouped by the first static URI segment. A group that contains more than half of the version's endpoints is split by the next static segment. Endpoints of such a group that have no next static segment stay in a resource named after the current segment. Splitting repeats until no group exceeds the threshold or until a large group cannot be split: all its endpoints have the same next static segment, or none of them has one.
